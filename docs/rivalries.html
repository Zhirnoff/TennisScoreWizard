New Rivalries

Every opponent has a story.

Choose a player before a singles match and Tennis Score Wizard builds your private Head-to-Head automatically — every meeting, result, pressure point, pattern, workout, and turning point in one place.

On court

Pick the opponent. Then just play.

Saved players appear in the match setup on Apple Watch. Guest keeps a match outside Rivalries; choosing a player places their short name directly on the live scoring screen.

No extra work during the match.

Your normal scoring taps already contain everything Rivalries needs. The result is attached to the selected player when the match is saved.

  • Create and manage players on iPhone or in Standalone
  • Fast delivery to Watch through the paired connection
  • Private iCloud synchronization across your devices
  • Assign an older saved match to a player later
The complete record

One player. Every angle.

Filter the rivalry by All Time, This Year, or Last 30 Days. Compare your latest five decided matches with the full series, then move from the overall score into the details that explain it.

Head-to-Head that goes beyond wins and losses.

Rivalries combines match outcomes with the same point-by-point intelligence already recorded by Tennis Score Wizard.

  • Matches, win rate, sets, games, and tie-breaks
  • Point, serve, return, and break-point performance
  • Current form and Last 5 versus All Time
  • Time on court, energy, and heart-rate summaries
  • Private notes that never appear in shared cards
Clear definitions

Deep statistics, explained simply.

Every metric uses match data you recorded on court. The in-app guide explains what each result means and when it is counted.

01 · HEAD-TO-HEAD

Your decided match record

Completed matches and stopped matches with a clear leader contribute to the series. Tied stopped matches remain neutral.

02 · RECENT FORM

Last 5 vs All Time

Compare the match win rate from your latest five decided meetings with the full selected period — no unexplained percentage-point shorthand.

03 · PRESSURE

Break points saved and converted

See how often you defended break points on serve and converted opportunities on return.

04 · COMEBACKS

Wins after losing Set 1

A comeback win is a decided match you won after losing the first completed set. The card shows both wins and qualifying chances.

05 · STREAKS

Current and best winning runs

Follow the latest sequence of decided results and the longest run of consecutive match wins in the selected period.

06 · MEETINGS

Open every recorded match

Review the score and full saved match statistics from the rivalry, or connect an older match to the correct player afterwards.

Share the series

Three cards. One rivalry story.

Share an ordered set of real cards: first the record and recent meetings, then match performance, and finally patterns, time, and workout context. Only sections with relevant recorded data are included.

✓

Private player profiles

Player names, Watch names, private notes, and match assignments stay on your devices and can synchronize through the private CloudKit database for your Apple Account. Private notes are never placed in Rivalry sharing cards.
