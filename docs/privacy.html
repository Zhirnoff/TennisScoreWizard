Tennis Score Wizard

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy covers Tennis Score Wizard (Standalone), Tennis Score Wizard Pro and this website. The apps keep your tennis records on your devices and can sync them through your Apple Account’s private iCloud database. No Tennis Score Wizard account is required, and the developer does not receive your match or Health data through a developer-operated server.

Website analytics, Apple services, files you choose to share and messages you send to support are explained separately below.

What the apps store

Saved records can contain player names, match rules, scores, dates, duration, point history, statistics and available workout summaries. Pro also supports manually entered results, optional court details and private match notes. These records are stored locally on Apple Watch and, with Pro, iPhone.

The apps do not require registration or an email address. They do not use advertising trackers or third-party app analytics. The developer does not sell match, player, note or Health data, or use it for advertising.

Private sync between your devices

When available, iCloud sync uses the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. Pro and Standalone use the same container so saved matches and player-library data can reach your devices under the same account. This storage is provided by Apple and counts towards your iCloud allowance.

Pro can also transfer saved matches and a Watch-safe player list directly between the paired iPhone and Watch through Watch Connectivity. Direct device transfer and iCloud sync are separate and can finish at different times.

By default, only the account owner can access the private CloudKit database. The developer cannot browse your private match records. Apple provides these services under its Privacy Policy and iCloud terms.

Changing your Apple Account

Each Apple Account has a separate history. Changing accounts does not merge histories or upload the previous account’s records to the new one. The app switches the visible local cache when it detects the change.

Before removing the previous account’s local cache from that device, records still waiting to sync are retained in an encrypted local recovery copy associated with that account. Changing accounts does not delete records already stored in either account’s private iCloud database.

Players and Rivalries

Player profiles may contain a full name, a shorter Watch name, a private note, an internal identifier and links to saved Singles matches. Your own player card stores the names used for you. Profiles and match assignments can sync through private iCloud.

The direct player list sent to Watch excludes private player notes and match assignments. Private player notes are also excluded from shared Rivalry text and image cards. Internal identifiers and deletion records help keep profiles consistent across devices.

Court details and private match notes

In Pro, you can enter a played result and add optional timing, surface, indoor or outdoor setting, venue, court and a personal note. These details are stored with the match and can follow its private sync paths. Manual entry does not reconstruct point events or Health measurements.

Court and venue names are typed as text. This feature does not request GPS location or search a map service. Favorite courts are stored on that iPhone and kept separate by Apple Account; selecting one does not copy another match’s note.

Court conditions and location can appear on a shared match image. Private match and Rivalry notes are excluded from those images. Review the preview before sharing, particularly if you do not want to disclose a venue.

Recorded points, replay and backups

A recorded point sequence may be saved with the match for Time Machine, Match Flow and related statistics. It can follow the same private sync paths as the result. Replay reads that saved sequence; it is not a camera or microphone recording and does not create points that were never recorded.

Pro can create reports and JSON or ZIP backups on your device. These files can contain personal match information and recorded workout summaries. You decide where to save or share them. A separate file can remain outside the app even if the original match is later deleted.

Supported files can be imported after a preview. Import does not duplicate existing match IDs or resurrect records previously deleted from the app.

Sending a match to another player

If you choose Send Match to Another iPhone, Pro creates a JSON snapshot from the recipient’s perspective. Player sides and results are reversed, and recorded point flow is included when available. Private notes, court context, Health data and player-library links are omitted.

The recipient can import the supported file into Pro. It is a separate copy, not a live shared record: later changes or deletions in your app do not remove a copy you already sent.

Optional Health and workout features

With your permission, the Watch app can record a tennis workout, show available live heart rate and save workout information through HealthKit. Match details may include duration, average and maximum heart rate and active energy when available.

Health access is optional and controlled by Apple’s permission system. You can score without it. The apps use available Health data for workout-related features, not advertising, and do not send it to a developer-operated server. Saved workout summaries can accompany a match through its private sync and backup paths.

When a network connection is used

Live scoring does not require an internet connection. Network access is used for Apple services such as private iCloud sync and optional App Store purchases, and when you choose to open external links or services. Those providers handle their services under their own terms.

Apple may process information needed to provide the App Store, purchases, iCloud and operating-system services. This is separate from the developer collecting match or Health data.

Sharing cards and saving images

You choose whether to share a result, Rivalry, recap, statistics, trend or supported match file. A card or text summary includes the information shown for that sharing option; a backup is a different, potentially more detailed file. Check the preview and destination.

Saving a card to Photos uses the requested Photos permission. Copies in Photos, another app or a recipient’s device are controlled there. Private match and player notes are not included in shared result or Rivalry cards.

Optional support payments

Optional Apple tips in Pro are processed by Apple’s in-app purchase system. The developer does not receive your card details. Apple may supply purchase and financial reports under its own terms. Tips do not unlock features or change data handling.

For optional cryptocurrency support, the app or website displays project wallet addresses and QR codes. You make any transfer in your own wallet. Public blockchains can disclose wallet addresses, amounts and transfer times; transfers are generally irreversible. The app does not attach match, player, note or Health data to the transfer.

Website analytics—not app analytics

This website uses GoatCounter for aggregate reports on page visits, referral sources, approximate locations, browsers, languages, screen sizes and App Store link clicks. This is separate from the apps: no matches, player profiles, notes or Health data are sent to website analytics.

GoatCounter processes IP addresses to estimate location and recognize repeat visits for up to eight hours. Its documentation states that IP addresses are not stored in its analytics database and that the counter does not use tracking cookies. Our site reports are aggregate, not individual pageview records.

Read GoatCounter’s privacy information. You can exclude or include this browser; the preference applies to that browser/profile only.

Deleting, reinstalling and keeping copies

Deleting a match inside the app sends that deletion through available device and iCloud sync. Delete All concerns the whole app history, not just a search or filter. Deletion records prevent a later backup import from restoring those deleted IDs.

Removing the app does not by itself delete matches already synced to iCloud. They can download after reinstallation under the same Apple Account. Records that never completed sync may exist only on the original device. Keep a separate exported backup if needed, and do not uninstall to troubleshoot before checking sync.

App history, Apple Health workouts, exported files and copies shared with others are separate. Deleting a match is not a promise that every external copy or related Health record has also been deleted.

When you contact support

If you email support, we receive your email address and what you choose to send. We use it to respond to and resolve your request. Do not include private match files, Health details or other personal information unless it is needed and you want to share it.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the apps or their data practices change. The latest published version is available on this page.
