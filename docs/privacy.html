Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to both Tennis Score Wizard and Tennis Score Wizard Companion. The apps are designed to keep tennis scoring, match history, match statistics, and workout information on your own devices. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data Collection

Tennis Score Wizard and Tennis Score Wizard Companion do not collect personal data from users. The apps do not require an account, login, registration, email address, or profile.

Match History, Match Stats, and Device Sync

Match history is stored locally on your Apple Watch and, when using Tennis Score Wizard Companion, on your paired iPhone. Automatic match stats, including points, serve, return, pressure, game flow, tie-break, and workout summary values, are stored locally as part of your saved match history. Tennis Score Wizard Companion may transfer match data directly between your Apple Watch and iPhone using Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. We do not receive or access your match history or match stats.

Apple Health and HealthKit

The apps can optionally request access to Apple Health to record tennis matches as workouts, display live heart rate during a recorded match, and show workout summary values such as average heart rate, maximum heart rate, active calories, and workout duration after the workout is saved.

Health Access Is Optional

Health access is optional and controlled through Apple’s permission system. If permission is granted, workout, heart rate, and active energy data are handled by Apple Health on your devices and used only to provide workout-related features. We do not receive, upload, sell, or share Health data.

Network Access

The apps do not require network access for core scoring functionality. They do not upload your tennis scores, match history, match stats, workout data, or heart rate data to developer-operated external servers.

Analytics and Tracking

The apps do not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking technologies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data, match history, match stats, workout data, heart rate data, or active energy data with third parties.

Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you voluntarily include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your request. This information is used only to respond to and resolve your support request.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the apps or their data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
